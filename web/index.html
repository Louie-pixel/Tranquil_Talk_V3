ON YOUR TERMS

Time & reminders

Choose how appointment times appear and which appointment emails you receive.

Reminders are scheduled 24 hours and one hour before accepted appointments, when those times are still ahead. Account verification and password recovery emails remain available.

Request a private reset link. Resetting your password ends existing sign-in sessions and appointment call access.

Reports are private to administrators. Use the report button on an expert profile or appointment to fill in the item.

Delete your account

This permanently removes your account, profile, appointments, authored articles and reports you submitted. Your active consultations will close. This cannot be undone.
